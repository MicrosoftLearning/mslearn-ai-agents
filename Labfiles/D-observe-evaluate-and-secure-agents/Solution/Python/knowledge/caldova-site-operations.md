# Caldova - Site Operations Handbook

Effective: January 2026
Owner: Manufacturing Operations Team

This handbook gives Caldova site staff the operational policies they need day to day: planning
desk hours, staff accounts and systems security, and how to handle regulated data. The internal
knowledge assistant is grounded on this document so staff can get quick, accurate answers.

========================================
PLANNING DESK AND STAFF AVAILABILITY
========================================

Planning Desk Hours:
- Monday-Friday: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM (site local time)
- Weekend cover: on-call planner for launch-critical programs only
- Site technical desk: opens 30 minutes before the planning desk and closes 30 minutes after

Core Hours for Staff:
All site staff must be contactable during core hours:
- Monday-Friday: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (local time)
- Respond to internal Teams messages within 30 minutes during core hours
- Trade or swap shifts through the staff scheduling app at least 48 hours in advance

========================================
STAFF ACCOUNTS AND SYSTEMS SECURITY
========================================

Every staff member has a Caldova account used for the manufacturing execution system (MES), the
materials portal, and Microsoft 365 (email, Teams, SharePoint).

Password Requirements:
- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
- Change every 90 days
- Cannot reuse the last 5 passwords
- No dictionary words or common patterns

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):
MFA is required for the MES, the materials portal, VPN access, and all Microsoft 365 apps.

Supported MFA methods:
- Microsoft Authenticator app (recommended)
- SMS text message (backup)
- Security key (for supervisor and admin accounts)
- Phone call (alternative)

Register at least two authentication methods and keep backup codes in a safe location.

Workstation Handling:
- Lock the workstation (Windows+L) whenever stepping away from a terminal
- Never share an MES login with another staff member
- Report a lost badge or suspected account compromise to the site supervisor immediately

========================================
REGULATED DATA HANDLING
========================================

Data Classification:
- PUBLIC: published product information, site addresses, general policies
- INTERNAL: staffing schedules, standard operating procedures, capacity reports
- CONFIDENTIAL: batch records, deviation reports, program schedules
- RESTRICTED: supplier pricing agreements, unreleased program plans, audit findings

Rules for Confidential and Restricted data:
- Access on a need-to-know basis only
- Never email batch records or audit findings outside Caldova
- Share documents through SharePoint or OneDrive links only, never personal cloud services
- Set expiration dates on shared links and review shares quarterly

========================================
CAPACITY AND PLANNING DESK
========================================

Caldova operates three manufacturing sites: Ashford, Brightwater, and Calderwood.
Staff at the planning desk:
- Confirm slot availability and headroom before accepting a capacity request
- Check internal headroom across all three sites before considering a contract manufacturer
- Record the approved request in the planning portal
