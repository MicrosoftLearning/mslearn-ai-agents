# Caldova - Contract Manufacturer Directory 2026

## Selecting a Contract Manufacturer

This directory helps planning and procurement teams choose a pre-qualified contract
manufacturing organization (CMO) when internal capacity cannot cover demand. The internal
knowledge assistant is grounded on this document.

Only pre-qualified CMOs may be engaged. Qualification is held by the supplier quality team and
reviewed annually.

---

## Understanding Transfer Tiers

A transfer tier describes the complexity of the work being moved, and sets the weekly rate.

**Tier Chart:**
- **Standard:** packaging and secondary operations, $50K per week
- **Advanced:** oral solid dose manufacturing, $120K per week
- **Premium:** sterile manufacturing, $300K per week

Priority sets a multiplier on top of the weekly rate:
- **Standard:** 1.00x, normal onboarding schedule
- **Expedited:** 1.25x, prioritized in the CMO queue
- **Fast track:** 1.75x, dedicated transfer team
- **Emergency:** 2.50x, out-of-hours mobilization, by exception only

Total cost is the weekly rate multiplied by the number of weeks, multiplied by the priority
factor.

## Halden Biologics

**Supplier code:** CMO-HAL
**Transfer tier:** Premium

The network's only pre-qualified sterile partner, and the sole external option when Brightwater
is full.

**Profile:**
- **Capability:** Sterile fill and finish, vial and prefilled syringe
- **Weekly rate:** $300K
- **Fast-track window:** 4 months to first commercial batch
- **Qualification status:** Qualified
- **Audit:** Last audited March 2026, no open findings
- **Capacity:** 2 dedicated suites, 1 available from Q3

**Best For:** Sterile shortfall, launch-critical sterile batches

**Constraint:** Longest onboarding in the directory; cannot support a 3-month window

## Norvent Pharma Services

**Supplier code:** CMO-NOR
**Transfer tier:** Advanced

The most flexible partner for oral solid dose work and the usual first call for a capacity gap
at Ashford.

**Profile:**
- **Capability:** Tableting, encapsulation, granulation
- **Weekly rate:** $120K
- **Fast-track window:** 3 months to first commercial batch
- **Qualification status:** Qualified
- **Audit:** Last audited January 2026, no open findings
- **Capacity:** 3 lines, 2 available from Q2

**Best For:** Oral solid dose shortfall, absorbing Ashford overflow

**Constraint:** No sterile capability

## Kestrel Contract Manufacturing

**Supplier code:** CMO-KES
**Transfer tier:** Standard

A packaging specialist, and the fastest partner to bring online.

**Profile:**
- **Capability:** Primary and secondary packaging, serialization
- **Weekly rate:** $50K
- **Fast-track window:** 6 weeks to first commercial batch
- **Qualification status:** Conditional - approved for packaging only, with additional testing
- **Audit:** Last audited November 2025, one finding closed February 2026
- **Capacity:** 4 packaging lines, 3 available immediately

**Best For:** Relieving the Ashford packaging bottleneck at short notice

**Constraint:** Conditional qualification; cannot take manufacturing work
