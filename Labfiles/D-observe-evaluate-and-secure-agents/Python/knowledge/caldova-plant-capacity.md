# Caldova - Plant Capacity Profile 2026

## Manufacturing Network Overview

Caldova operates three manufacturing sites. This profile gives planning and procurement teams
the capability, capacity, and constraint information they need to answer questions about where
a product can be made and how much headroom each site has. The internal knowledge assistant is
grounded on this document.

The V2 program (compound code CAL-204) is currently forecast to run a 7% capacity gap across
the network. Use the figures below when assessing whether that gap can be closed internally.

---

## Ashford

**Site code:** SITE-ASH
**Primary capability:** Oral solid dose

Ashford is the network's highest-volume oral solid dose site and carries most of the routine
commercial schedule.

**Profile:**
- **Lines:** 4 (2 tableting, 1 encapsulation, 1 packaging)
- **Rated capacity:** 180 batches per quarter
- **Committed capacity:** 172 batches per quarter
- **Available headroom:** 8 batches per quarter
- **Typical changeover:** 36 hours between products
- **Qualification status:** Qualified for commercial supply
- **Constraint:** Packaging line B is the bottleneck; artwork changes add 4 weeks

**Best for:** High-volume tablet and capsule production, launch stock build

---

## Brightwater

**Site code:** SITE-BRW
**Primary capability:** Sterile fill and finish

Brightwater is the only site in the network qualified for sterile manufacturing, which makes
it both the most capable and the most constrained.

**Profile:**
- **Lines:** 2 (1 vial filling, 1 inspection and packaging)
- **Rated capacity:** 90 batches per quarter
- **Committed capacity:** 88 batches per quarter
- **Available headroom:** 2 batches per quarter
- **Typical changeover:** 72 hours between products, including sterilization
- **Qualification status:** Qualified for commercial supply
- **Constraint:** Sterile suite availability; no second filling line until 2027

**Best for:** Sterile vial products, launch-critical sterile batches

---

## Calderwood

**Site code:** SITE-CLD
**Primary capability:** Granulation and blending

Calderwood supplies intermediate material to the other two sites and holds the most spare
capacity in the network.

**Profile:**
- **Lines:** 3 (2 granulation, 1 blending)
- **Rated capacity:** 120 batches per quarter
- **Committed capacity:** 96 batches per quarter
- **Available headroom:** 24 batches per quarter
- **Typical changeover:** 24 hours between products
- **Qualification status:** Qualified for commercial supply
- **Constraint:** No packaging capability; output must ship to Ashford or Brightwater

**Best for:** Intermediate manufacture, absorbing upstream demand peaks

## Network Summary

| Site | Capability | Rated | Committed | Headroom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ashford | Oral solid dose | 180 | 172 | 8 |
| Brightwater | Sterile fill and finish | 90 | 88 | 2 |
| Calderwood | Granulation and blending | 120 | 96 | 24 |

**Total rated capacity:** 390 batches per quarter
**Total committed:** 356 batches per quarter
**Total headroom:** 34 batches per quarter

Headroom is not interchangeable between sites. Calderwood holds most of the spare capacity but
cannot package, and Brightwater is the only sterile site. A sterile shortfall cannot be covered
internally and must go to a contract manufacturer.

---

## Slot Types

- **Production:** standard batch manufacturing windows
- **Packaging:** primary and secondary packaging windows
- **Changeover:** line cleaning and format changes between products
- **Qualification:** validation and stability sampling runs

Qualification slots are protected. They cannot be shortened or reassigned to create production
time, even for launch-critical programs.
