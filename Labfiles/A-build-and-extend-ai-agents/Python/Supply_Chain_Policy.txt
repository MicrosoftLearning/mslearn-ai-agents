CALDOVA PHARMACEUTICALS
SUPPLY CHAIN AND CAPACITY POLICY

Effective Date: January 2026
Version: 2.0

====================
1. CAPACITY REQUESTS
====================

Planning teams raise a capacity request when a program needs production time beyond the
committed schedule.

Process:
1. Raise the request in the planning portal at https://planning.caldova.example
2. State the program, the site, the number of weeks, and the required start date
3. The planning desk checks internal capacity across all three sites first
4. If internal capacity cannot cover the request, the desk evaluates contract manufacturers

Review Windows:
- Standard requests: reviewed within 5 business days
- Expedited requests: reviewed within 2 business days
- Requests inside 30 days of the required start: escalated to the site planning lead

An agent or assistant may draft a capacity request. Drafts are always reviewed by a planner
before anything is scheduled. Nothing is reserved and no spend is committed until a planner
approves the request.

====================
2. CONTRACT MANUFACTURERS
====================

Where internal capacity is short, production may be transferred to a pre-qualified contract
manufacturing organization (CMO).

Transfer Tiers:
- Standard: packaging and secondary operations, $50K per week
- Advanced: oral solid dose manufacturing, $120K per week
- Premium: sterile manufacturing, $300K per week

Priority Levels:
- Standard: 1.00x, normal onboarding schedule
- Expedited: 1.25x, prioritized in the CMO queue
- Fast track: 1.75x, dedicated transfer team
- Emergency: 2.50x, out-of-hours mobilization by exception only

Cost is calculated as the weekly rate multiplied by the number of weeks, multiplied by the
priority factor. For example, 5 weeks of premium capacity at expedited priority is
5 x $300K x 1.25 = $1,875K.

Only pre-qualified CMOs may be engaged. Qualification is held by the supplier quality team and
reviewed annually.

====================
3. PRODUCTION SLOTS
====================

Caldova operates three manufacturing sites: Ashford, Brightwater, and Calderwood.

Slot Types:
- Production: standard batch manufacturing windows
- Packaging: primary and secondary packaging windows
- Changeover: line cleaning and format changes between products
- Qualification: validation and stability sampling runs

Slot Request Rules:
- Confirm the slot is open with the planning desk before committing materials
- Request a slot at least 4 weeks before the required start date
- Changeover time is reserved automatically either side of a production slot
- Qualification slots cannot be shortened to create production time

====================
4. MATERIALS AND INBOUND LOGISTICS
====================

Reorder Thresholds:
- Reorder when on-hand inventory falls below 10 units and weekly consumption is above 15 units
- Flag for review when inventory is above 20 units and weekly consumption is below 5 units
- Critical materials should never fall below 5 units on hand

Inbound Handling:
- Record every delivery against its purchase order on receipt
- Quarantine incoming material until quality release
- Report short or damaged shipments to the supplier within 48 hours of delivery

Lead Times:
- Standard materials: 2 to 3 weeks
- Sterile components: 6 weeks
- Printed packaging: 4 weeks, longer if artwork changes

====================
5. QUALITY AND BATCH RELEASE
====================

Deviations:
- Log every deviation in the quality system on the day it is observed
- Deviations affecting a released batch are escalated to the quality lead immediately
- No batch ships before quality release, regardless of launch pressure

Batch Release:
- Release requires complete batch records, in-specification test results, and a quality signature
- Target release time is 10 business days from batch completion
- Expedited release may be requested for launch-critical batches but never shortens testing

Change Control:
- Any change to a process, material, supplier, or system requires an approved change control
- Assistants and agents must not make changes in production systems or supplier portals
- Agents may draft requests and summarize records for a person to review

====================
6. SUPPLIER QUALIFICATION
====================

Qualification Status:
- Qualified: approved for routine supply
- Conditional: approved for named programs only, with additional testing
- Under review: not available for new work until review closes

Requirements:
- A current quality agreement must be in place before any order is raised
- Audit findings must be closed before qualification is renewed
- Requalification is required after any change of manufacturing site

====================
8. COST REVIEW
====================

Cost Approval:
- Transfers above $500K require sign-off from the site planning lead
- Transfers above $2,000K require sign-off from the VP of Manufacturing
- Emergency priority always requires VP sign-off, whatever the value

Review:
- Every transfer is reviewed against its estimate at completion
- Variances above 10 percent are reported to the planning review meeting
