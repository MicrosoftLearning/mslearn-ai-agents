# Caldova - Supplier and Materials Guide

Effective: January 2026
Owner: Supply Chain Team

This guide gives planning and materials staff the supplier, reorder, and supplier-returns
information they need to keep production running and to return defective material correctly.
The internal knowledge assistant is grounded on this document.

========================================
KEY SUPPLIERS
========================================

Active pharmaceutical ingredient (CAL-204 API):
- Supplier: Meridian Fine Chemicals
- Lead time: 8 weeks
- Minimum order: 25 kg
- Contact: orders@meridianfine.example

Sterile components (vials, stoppers, filter cartridges):
- Supplier: Aldercroft Components
- Lead time: 6 weeks
- Minimum order: 5,000 units per component
- Contact: wholesale@aldercroft.example

Packaging materials (blister film, carton board, label stock, foil laminate):
- Supplier: Penmoor Packaging
- Lead time: 4 weeks, longer if artwork changes
- Minimum order: no minimum for stock items; 10,000 units for printed components
- Contact: b2b@penmoor.example

========================================
REORDER RULES
========================================

Reorder Thresholds:
- Reorder when on-hand inventory falls below 10 units and weekly consumption is above 15 units
- Flag for review when on-hand inventory is above 20 units and weekly consumption is below 5 units
- Critical materials (CAL-204 API, sterile vials) should never fall below 5 units on hand

Ordering Process:
1. Check current on-hand levels in the materials portal
2. Compare against the reorder thresholds above
3. Raise a purchase order to the relevant supplier, respecting the minimum order quantity
4. Record the expected delivery date and follow up if it is late

Seasonal Notes:
- Order sterile components a full quarter ahead; Aldercroft lead times extend in Q4
- Increase packaging stock ahead of any launch build

========================================
RETURNS TO SUPPLIER
========================================

Defective Material:
- Log every defective item in the materials portal with the material code, batch, and a short description
- Batch defective returns weekly and ship them back under the supplier's RMA (Return Material Authorization) process
- Request an RMA number from the supplier before shipping; never ship without one

Quality Claims:
- Material rejected at incoming inspection is quarantined and logged as a deviation
- Procurement recovers the cost from the supplier under the quality agreement

Damaged-in-Transit:
- Photograph damaged shipments before unpacking further
- Report to the supplier within 48 hours of delivery to qualify for replacement
