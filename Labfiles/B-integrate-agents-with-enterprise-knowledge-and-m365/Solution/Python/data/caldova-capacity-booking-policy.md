# Caldova - Capacity Request Policy

Effective: January 2026
Owner: Network Planning Team

This policy tells planning staff how to raise and approve capacity requests, and how contract
manufacturing costs are calculated. The internal knowledge assistant is grounded on this
document.

========================================
CAPACITY REQUESTS
========================================

Standard Request Window:
- Requests with a complete program brief: reviewed within 5 business days
- Expedited requests: reviewed within 2 business days
- Requests inside 30 days of the required start: escalated to the site planning lead

Internal First:
- The planning desk checks headroom across all three sites before considering a CMO
- Headroom is not interchangeable between sites; a sterile shortfall cannot be covered by
  spare granulation capacity at Calderwood
- Where internal capacity covers the request, no transfer cost applies

Incomplete Requests:
- Requests without a program, site, duration, and required start date are returned
- Requests naming a CMO that is not pre-qualified are returned to procurement

========================================
PRODUCTION SLOTS
========================================

Caldova operates three manufacturing sites: Ashford, Brightwater, and Calderwood.

Slot Types:
- Production: standard batch manufacturing windows
- Packaging: primary and secondary packaging windows
- Changeover: line cleaning and format changes between products
- Qualification: validation and stability sampling runs

Slot Request Rules:
- Confirm the slot is open with the planning desk before committing materials
- Request a slot at least 4 weeks before the required start date
- Changeover time is reserved automatically either side of a production slot
- Qualification slots cannot be shortened to create production time

========================================
CONTRACT MANUFACTURING COSTS
========================================

Caldova transfers packaging, oral solid dose, and sterile work to pre-qualified partners.

Transfer Tiers:
- Standard tier (packaging and secondary operations): $50K per week
- Advanced tier (oral solid dose manufacturing): $120K per week
- Premium tier (sterile manufacturing): $300K per week

Priority Multipliers:
- Standard priority: 1.00x
- Expedited priority (prioritized in the CMO queue): 1.25x
- Fast track priority (dedicated transfer team): 1.75x
- Emergency priority (out-of-hours mobilization): 2.50x

Example Calculation:
- 5 weeks of premium capacity at expedited priority = 5 x $300K x 1.25 = $1,875K

Approval:
- Transfers above $500K require sign-off from the site planning lead
- Transfers above $2,000K require sign-off from the VP of Manufacturing
- Emergency priority always requires VP sign-off, whatever the value

========================================
DRAFTING AND APPROVAL
========================================

- An assistant may draft a capacity request, an estimate, or a summary
- A draft does not reserve capacity and does not commit spend
- A planner reviews and approves every request before anything is scheduled
- Agents must not write to production systems or supplier portals
